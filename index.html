How this is worked out

Each month is modelled as one typical day. Solar output comes from average sun hours for your city and mounting, and is clipped hour by hour at the inverter cap. Each hour, solar goes first to the house, then to the battery, then out to the grid. The battery then covers evening use. Savings rise with electricity prices and fall slowly as panels degrade. Future savings are not discounted.

  • No weather variation: real years will be better or worse.
  • Sun hours are long-term averages from the EU's PVGIS service for each capital city. Kit prices are provisional estimates.
  • Standby draw from the inverter and battery is not included.
  • Daily supply charges are unchanged by plug-in systems and are left out.
